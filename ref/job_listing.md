General Summary:

At Qualcomm XR Research, we develop state-of-the-art XR technologies that enable exceptional AR/VR/MR user experiences.

We are looking for a Staff Engineer to own and drive validation of perception systems for XR (AR/VR/MR) technologies. This role focuses on validating SLAM and other perception algorithms, defining meaningful KPIs, building scalable test automation, and performing deep failure analysis. The role requires strong mathematical fundamentals, hands-on Python expertise, and the ability to communicate technical issues clearly to senior leadership and cross-functional stakeholders.

Key Responsibilities

Perception & Algorithm Validation

- Validate SLAM (visual / visual-inertial / multi-sensor) and other algorithms across XR use cases.

- Design scenario-driven validation strategies covering accuracy, robustness, stability, and failure modes.

- Evaluate performance across sensors, calibrations, environments, and system configurations.

- Define and evolve perception KPIs (pose accuracy, drift, stability/jitter, robustness, reconstruction quality).

- Perform mathematical and statistical analysis of logs, trajectories, and 3D outputs.

- Identify and implement automation opportunities to improve test coverage, repeatability, and efficiency.

- Perform preliminary root-cause analysis of perception failures and regressions.

- Support algorithm and system teams with actionable debugging insights.

Required Skills & Qualifications

- Strong foundation in mathematics (linear algebra, geometry, probability, optimization).

- Strong proficiency in Python for automation, analysis, and tooling.

- Solid understanding of SLAM algorithms and perception pipelines.

- Strong knowledge of sensor calibration (camera, IMU, multi-sensor systems).

- Experience with large log analysis, offline processing, and metrics generation.

- Strong communication skills, with the ability to explain complex technical issues clearly.

- Proven ability to work across teams and support stakeholder debugging needs.

Preferred

- 9+ years of relevant industry experience in perception systems, computer vision, robotics, or XR technologies.

- M.Tech / MS in Computer Science, Robotics, Electrical Engineering, Applied Mathematics, or a related technical field.

- Experience with XR, AR/VR/MR, robotics, or autonomous systems.

- Familiarity with tools such as GTSAM, Ceres, OpenCV, ROS, or similar libraries.

- Experience with ground-truth systems (motion capture, GNSS, fiducials).

- Experience influencing validation strategy at a system or org level.
